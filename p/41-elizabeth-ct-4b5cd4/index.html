3. Pricing

I usually recommend a range rather than one number, and we set it together. A listing gets the most attention in its first two weeks on the market, so the opening price matters more than any later adjustment. Starting high to leave room to negotiate usually means fewer showings and a price cut later.

Five of the six sales are ranch-style homes; they closed between $720,000 and $775,000. 4 Gail Pl sold above that range at $825,000; it is a colonial. We will set the range together on Friday.

5. Before we list

  • Helpful to have on hand: the deed or title policy, the most recent survey, your latest mortgage statement, this year's tax bill, and permits or certificates for any work done on the house.
  • Secaucus asks for two inspections before closing: a smoke alarm, carbon monoxide alarm and fire extinguisher certificate from the Bureau of Fire Prevention (the fee is in the estimate above), and a sale certification from the Construction Department, which asks that any open permits be closed first. The town recommends scheduling both about two weeks before closing.
  • Once a contract is signed, New Jersey gives both sides an attorney review period, usually three business days, before it is binding.
  • FEMA's flood map places 41 Elizabeth Ct in Zone X, an area of minimal flood hazard, outside the special flood hazard area. New Jersey's flood disclosure law, in effect since March 2024, still has every seller answer the flood questions on the property condition disclosure statement, including any past water in the house, so we will go through those together.
